Callout Examples

Examples from the Alberta Design System v4.0.0+ showing different callout types and use cases.

Example 1: Application Confirmation (Success)

This pattern confirms that an application was successfully submitted.

You have completed the application

You will receive a copy of the confirmation to the email {{ userEmail }}

Confirmation number: {{ confirmationNumber }}

Go back to the dashboard, or direct your user somewhere else useful.

Other information about what was just completed, other tertiary information, and/or contact information.
Phone: {{ phoneNumber }}
Email: {{ supportEmail }}

Go to application Back to dashboard

Example 2: Information Callout

Use for important information that users should be aware of.

Please note that processing times may vary depending on the complexity of your application. You will receive updates via email as your application progresses through our review process.

If you need to make changes to your application, please contact our support team within 24 hours of submission.

Example 3: Warning Callout

Use to warn users about potential issues or important considerations.

Once you submit this application, you will not be able to make changes online. Any modifications will require contacting our support team and may result in processing delays.

Please ensure all information is accurate before proceeding.

 Submit Application Review Again

Example 4: Error Callout

Use when an error has occurred and user action is required.

We encountered an error while processing your submission. This could be due to a temporary system issue or missing required information.

Please check that all required fields are completed and try again. If the problem persists, contact our support team.

 Try Again Contact Support

Example 5: Emergency Callout

Use for urgent, critical information that requires immediate attention.

Our systems are currently undergoing scheduled maintenance. Some services may be temporarily unavailable.

Estimated completion: January 15, 2025 at 3:00 AM MST

For urgent matters, please call our emergency hotline at {{ phoneNumber }}
