Usage Guidelines

When to use different footer configurations:

  • Basic Footer: Simple applications or pages with minimal navigation needs
  • Meta Links Footer: Applications requiring quick access to legal/policy information
  • Navigation Footer: Main government websites with extensive content and services
  • Multi-Column Footer: Large websites with many services, better organization on desktop
  • Department Footer: Specific department sites with focused navigation

Best Practices:

  • Always include the Government of Alberta branding and copyright
  • Keep meta links focused on essential items (Privacy, Accessibility, Contact)
  • Use appropriate max content width for your application layout
  • Consider responsive behavior when choosing column counts
  • Group related navigation links logically
  • Test footer usability on different screen sizes
